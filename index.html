Spatial Pattern of Life Expectancy

How to read this dashboard

This dashboard visualises life expectancy and mortality indicators for Australian Statistical Area Level 4 (SA4) regions from 2016 to 2023. The following key terms are used throughout:

  • Life Expectancy (LE): The average number of years a newborn is expected to live if current age-specific death rates remain constant. In this dashboard, LE values are shown as rolling 3-year windows (e.g. 2021–2023) to smooth annual fluctuations.
  • Crude Mortality Rate: The total number of deaths per 100,000 people in a region, unadjusted for age.
  • Age-Standardised Mortality Rate (ASMR): Adjusts for age distribution differences across regions, enabling fair comparisons.
  • SA4 Region: “Statistical Area Level 4” — large geographical units representing communities of 100,000+ people.
  • Rolling 3-Year Window: A smoothing method where data are averaged over three-year periods to reduce volatility.

Use the window selector below the map to compare rolling 3-year periods; hover regions for details.

Data Sources

  • ABS Life Tables by SA4 (persons/male/female), rolling windows 2016–2023.
  • ABS Age-standardised Mortality (per 100k), 2016–2023.
  • AIHW (Australian Institute of Health and Welfare) National Mortality Database, 2016–2023.
  • ABS Estimated Resident Population (ERP) – SA4 (for bubble sizing / rates).
  • ABS ASGS 2021 – SA4 boundaries (TopoJSON derived).

Methods & Notes

  • Life expectancy shown as rolling 3-year windows to smooth volatility.
  • Map uses Mercator projection with tuned scale/center to include Tasmania.
  • Interactions: SA4 selection (map → charts), window selector, histogram brush.
  • Units: years / per 100k; benchmark rules show national averages.
